Late joining — yes, it is possible. Pick the shape.

Today the docs close the door after phase 1: a phone that arrives later is put on membership = "joiningClosed". Opening it up is a domain change — guard T4 in design/state-machine.md, invariant I4 wording in design/domain-model.md, and § 3.1 here. Nothing in the phase machine actually depends on a frozen roster; the only thing a late arrival misses is what was already computed before they existed: the selection tally (fixed on entry to phase 4) and the group partition (fixed on entry to phase 5, I8).

Your idea beats both of mine. Auto-placing the late arrival into the currently smallest group (option C below) needs no facilitator action, no new UI, and no “you are in no group” dead end. It is now the recommendation. The only thing it gives up is the formation aim — a late arrival has no value selection to optimise against (they were not there in phase 3), so there is nothing to optimise, and the sizing rule (sizes differ by at most one) is exactly what “smallest group” preserves.
C Join in any phase; after formation, auto-placed into the smallest group recommended
  • Everything in A, plus: if groups already exist, joining also emits ParticipantAddedToGroup (System, part of the same join) into the group with the fewest members — ties broken randomly.
  • Sizing rule survives: groups still differ by at most one member. No values are re-dealt — the value–group assignment stays frozen, so I12 and the presentation walk are untouched.
  • The new member is never the scribe (I9 appointed one at phase-6 entry); if the group already submitted, it stays submitted — an extra member changes nothing about I11.
  • Same rule in every phase from 5 on, so there is no special case to reason about: whenever groups exist, a joiner lands in one.
  • I8 changes from “membership never changes” to “the partition and the value assignment are formed once; a participant who joins later is placed into the smallest group”.
A Join in any phase, no group after formation
  • T4 guard drops the phase condition; joiningClosed disappears from the protocol.
  • Phase 2: answers the currently posed question like everyone else. Phase 3: submits their ten values, counted in the tally.
  • Phases 4 and 7 and 9 are watch-only anyway — identical experience.
  • Arriving in phase 5+ means no group (I8 keeps the partition frozen): their phone mirrors the presenter content through phases 5–7.
  • Phase 8: votes with the full allotment of the open round — I13 is per participant, so this needs no change.
  • Cost: one guard, one wording change, one FE state ("you are not in a group"). No new command, no relaxed invariant.
B A, plus the facilitator can place them in a group
  • Everything in A, and a new facilitator command AddParticipantToGroup / event ParticipantAddedToGroup, allowed in phases 5–7.
  • I8 softens from "groups are fixed" to "the partition is fixed; the facilitator may add a late arrival to one group".
  • Needs a facilitator control (group picker), a new rejection path, and scribe rules stay untouched (the new member is a plain member).
  • Real benefit only if late arrivals are common enough that sitting out group work would hurt the workshop.
